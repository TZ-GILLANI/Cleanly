🐙 Delete your Cleano account

App: Cleano  ·  Developer: TZ Gillani Kft., 4032 Debrecen, Bolyai utca 11. Fsz. 1. ajtó, Hungary

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Cleano app and log in.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the home screen to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

Email info@tzmanufacturing.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what we must keep

DataWhat happens
Name, email, phone number, address, passwordDeleted permanently
Saved payment card reference (held at Stripe, never on our servers)Deleted
Device push-notification tokenDeleted
Your reviews and messagesAnonymised — the text remains but is no longer linked to you
Completed bookings and invoicesKept for 8 years, as Hungarian accounting law requires (Act C of 2000, §169). Retained only for tax and accounting purposes, not for marketing.

Please note: deletion cannot be undone, and an account with a cleaning appointment still in progress must wait until that appointment is completed or cancelled.

Questions

Contact info@tzmanufacturing.com. See also our Privacy Policy.

Fiók törlése — magyarul

1. lehetőség: nyisd meg a Cleano appot, jelentkezz be, koppints a nevedre a főoldalon (Profil), majd lent a Fiók törlése gombra. A törlés azonnal megtörténik.

2. lehetőség: írj az info@tzmanufacturing.com címre a fiókodhoz tartozó e-mail-címről, „Fiók törlése” tárggyal. 30 napon belül töröljük.

Törlésre kerül: név, e-mail, telefonszám, cím, jelszó, kártyareferencia, push-token. Anonimizálva megmarad: értékelések és üzenetek szövege. Megőrizzük 8 évig: a teljesített foglalások és számlák adatai, a számvitelről szóló 2000. évi C. törvény 169. §-a alapján.
